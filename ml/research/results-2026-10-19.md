# Real-market model assessment — 2026-10-19

Training on real data produced mixed results, with no stable edge established. Both
sequence slots remained worse than predicting no change at all four horizons. The
180-session research ensemble improved pooled MAE, but failed the separate final
temporal assessment. No candidate was promoted and no 90% accuracy claim is justified.

## Fixed protocol

- Six preselected surviving liquid equities: RELIANCE.NS, TCS.NS, INFY.NS,
  HDFCBANK.NS, AAPL and MSFT. These instruments were selected before this run.
- Yahoo Finance adjusted daily OHLCV, requested 2016-10-17 inclusive through
  2026-10-17 exclusive. Indian stocks supplied 2,473 sessions each; US stocks
  supplied 2,514 each. Exchange-local final input session: 2026-10-16.
  No downloaded rows were excluded as invalid.
- Separate 7/30/90/180-observed-session labels; these are not calendar deadlines.
- Three chronological folds, at least 1,500 prior sessions, origins one horizon
  apart per instrument before fixed-seed sampling, at most 24 origins per fold.
- Two workers, fixed existing model parameters, no tuning or weight selection
  against assessment outcomes. All source hashes and settings were frozen before scoring.
- Twenty-four sequence fits, 236 per-origin RF fits (plus their internal validation
  fits), eight saved research candidates. Different horizons have different origins
  and sample counts; their pooled scores are not paired comparisons.
- Final sequence candidates use only labels resolved strictly before the final
  fold start. RF follows normal serving: per-origin fits use then-resolved labels,
  including earlier final-fold labels once observed (prequential assessment).

[Machine-readable aggregate evidence](results-2026-10-19.json) includes per-fold and
per-ticker results, actual source hashes, package versions, candidate hashes,
fit cutoffs and direction/coverage counts. [Configuration](real-world-2026-10-19.json)
and [workflow](README.md) document reproduction. Raw inputs and per-origin returns
remain ignored locally; provider data rights are separate from the source-code license.

## Pooled assessment

MAE is in percentage points of adjusted forward return. Signed skill is
`1 - MAE / MAE(no change)`; negative means worse than no change. Direction hits
are correct/nonzero served-price forecasts, not probability calibration. Up/down/
flat base rates and counts are in the JSON. Cross-stock observations are correlated.

The historical LSTM/GAN names identify deterministic XGBoost sequence-feature
slots, not neural networks. Ensemble rows recombine recorded member returns
offline with production weights and eligibility; they omit final cent rounding.
7/90/180-session refitted sequence artifacts are research candidates, while the
deployed sequence bundle remains 30 sessions.

## Limits and next evidence

Yahoo adjusted histories may be revised and are not a point-in-time dataset.
The selected current large caps introduce survivorship and selection bias.
Cross-stock returns and market regimes reduce effective independent sample size.
Assessment begins in late 2022 and therefore does not test 2008 or 2020 forecast
origins. No unseen-instrument holdout, real-time prospective evaluation, costs,
spreads, slippage, liquidity, position sizing or portfolio strategy P&L is included.
Confidence and legacy breakdown fields are heuristic scores; they do not measure
fundamental/news evidence or a probability of a correct forecast.

An eventual commercial product needs provider permission and independent
prospective evidence with clear target/horizon, baseline, coverage, uncertainty
and strategy costs. A 90% aspiration or one favorable aggregate cannot serve as
a fee trigger. There is no fee or paywall in this change.

  separately use synthetic data to test numerical, availability and cache invariants.

  histories: both horizons retained 48/48 coverage and all predictions matched
  within absolute return tolerance `1e-12` ([derived regression metrics](rsi-regression-2026-10-19.json)).
  The numerical correction fixes constructed boundary cases; this sample did not
  gain forecast accuracy from it.

  RF can now retain the actual current origin for sustained gains or flat prices.
- Exact label purging, directional denominators, cache hash/range checks, worker

- Independent review recomputed all pooled metrics and verified source/dataset/
  origin/candidate hashes, exact horizon labels, non-overlap and pre-fold label
  cutoffs. All eight research candidates failed serving readiness as intended;
  the two default bundles remained ready and byte-identical to the baseline.
